Summary
    Anaphylaxis may be encountered due to the large number of medications and substances to which a patient is exposed during cardiac procedures. Anaphylaxis may be difficult to diagnose due to multiple other causes of cardiopulmonary instability. Although cardiopulmonary bypass has been suggested as a therapy there are also case reports of refractory hypotension on bypass. The mainstay of treatment for anaphylaxis is epinephrine for mast cell stabilization.

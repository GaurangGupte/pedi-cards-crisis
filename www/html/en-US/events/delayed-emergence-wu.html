Initial Workup
  • If currently intubated, keep endotracheal tube in place. If extubated, consider bag mask ventilation
  • Ensure adequate blood pressure, heart rate/rhythm, and oxygen saturation
  • Check patient temperature and use active warming devices if necessary
  • Verify anesthetic agents have been discontinued
  • Ensure adequate reversal of neuromuscular blockade by checking train-of-four and re-dosing reversal agents if indicated
  • Review sedative medications administered during the anesthetic and possible dosing errors. Consider reversal of opioids and/or benzodiazepines if indicated (flumazenil, naloxone)

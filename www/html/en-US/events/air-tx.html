Treatment
  • Stop CPB; Clamp lines and shut off suction lines
  • Steep Trendelenburg (head DOWN)
  • 100% FiO2
  • Cool patient, ice on head
  • Remove arterial cannula to de-air patient, de-air circuit
  • Retrograde regional cerebral perfusion:
    • Connect arterial line to SVC cannula or RA cannula, use pump suckers for blood return
    • Flow at 15cc/kg/min (peds), 500-800 cc/min (adult) until air is gone
  • Resume antegrade perfusion
    • Maintain deep hypothermia (18-24oC) for 30-60 min
    • Keep flows on CPB as high as possible, induce hypertension with medications as necessary
    • Consider steroids
    • Maintain PaCO2 35-45, FiO2 100% until 6 hours post
    • Deepen anesthetic, consider barbituates
  • Slowly warm to 34-35oC
    • Keep temperature gradient small (less than 8oC)

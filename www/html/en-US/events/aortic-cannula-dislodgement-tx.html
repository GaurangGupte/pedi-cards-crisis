Treatment
  • Support vitals with inotrops + vasopressors
  • Head down to minimize air embolism risk
  • Monitor EBL in surgical field
  • Surgical team tasks
    • Occlude cannula site
    • If on CPB: Reinsert cannula at original or other site
    • If not CPB: Repair site and assess for futher injury
  • Perfusion Tasks
    • Stop pump
    • Clamp aortic line
    • Clamp venous line to avoid draining on CPB
    • Clamp vent if applicable
    • Can perfuse retrograde thru venous line if pre or post CPB
    • Provide new cannula to surgical team for reinsertion

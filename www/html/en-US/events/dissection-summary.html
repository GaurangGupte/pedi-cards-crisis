Summary
    Intraoperative iatrogenic aortic dissection is a rare but potentially fatal complication. It occurs in 0.12-0.35% of adult cardiac surgery cases. Known predisposing factors include connective tissue disease, hypertension, aortic pathology, atherosclerosis, and preoperative steroids, but can occur in the absence of disease.
    Types
    • Type A (ascending or arch) vs. Type B (descending)
    • Type will determine surgical intervention

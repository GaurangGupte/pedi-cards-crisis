Treatment
  • Check that heparin was administered
    • Ideally use a CVL
    • Confirm aspiration of blood and flushed completely
  • Give additional heparin bolus
    • (1/2 or same heparin dose as initial dose)
  • Recheck ACT on same or new machine
  • Transfuse FFP - 10 ml/kg OR Give ATIII (antithrombin) concentrate: 10-50 units/kg empirically
  • Recheck ACT

Summary
Electrical failure may be due to CPB console fault or failure of supply to the CPB machine wall socket. Priority is assuring pump operation via battery backup or hand-cranking. Separation from bypass should occur as soon as possible with ongoing electrical failure. The anesthesiologist will closely work with perfusionist and surgical team to assure adequate monitoring, assist with lighting, and optimize conditions for separation from bypass. If electrical failure is widespread, additional actions to address anesthesia machine/equipment failure are required.

Other Considerations
Intraoperative iatrogenic aortic dissection is a rare but potentially fatal complication. It occurs in 0.12-0.35% of adult cardiac surgery cases.  Known predisposing factors include connective tissue disease, hypertension, aortic pathology, atherosclerosis, and preoperative steroids, but can occur in the absence of disease. The risk is increased with large aortas and/or hypertension

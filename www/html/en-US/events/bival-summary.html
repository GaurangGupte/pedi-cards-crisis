Summary
    Indications
    • Cath Lab
      • Heparin-induced thrombocytopenia (HIT)
      • h/o HIT (regardless of antibody status)
      • Consider for: AT3 deficiency, heparin-refractory thrombosis
    • Cardiac Surgery with CPB
      • HIT
    • FDA-approved - For use with aspirin in adult patients:
      • With unstable angina undergoing percutaneous transluminal angioplasty (PTCA)
      • Undergoing percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI) with provisional use of glycoprotein IIb/IIIa (GPI) as in the REPLACE-2 study
      • With, or at risk of, heparin-induced thrombocytopenia (HIT) or heparin-induced thrombocytopenia and thrombosis syndrome (HITTS), undergoing PCI
    Pharmacology
    • Pharmakokinetics/Pharmacodynamics
      • Drug Class: direct thrombin inhibitor
      • Co-factors/antagonists: none
      • Metabolism: proteolytic cleavage 80%, renal 20%
      • Half-life:
        • Normal renal function - moderate dysfunction: 25-34 minutes
        • Severe renal dysfunction: 57 minutes
        • Dialysis-dependent: 3.5 hours
        • Pediatric patients: 15-18 minutes (age dependent)
      • Monitoring: ACT, aPTT
    • Dosing
      • Cath Lab
        • Bolus: 0.75 mg/kg, additional 0.3 mg/kg if needed
        • Infusion: 1.75 mg/kg/hr
        • For STEMI: consider continuing infusion post-cath (up to 4H)
      • Cardiac Surgery with CPB
        • Bolus: 1.5 mg/kg, additional 0.5-1 mg/kg if needed
        • Infusion: 2.5 mg/kg/hr
        • Pump Prime: 50mg

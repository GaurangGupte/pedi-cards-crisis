Treatment
  • Epinephrine boluses (1-10 mcg/kg IV) as needed and/or infusion
  • Volume as necessary
  • If refractory hypotension, consider vasopressin 0.01 units/kg IV
  • Stop all other medication administration if possible
  • Steroid: methylprednisolone 2mg/kg (MAX 100mg)
  • Histamine blockade: Diphenhydramine 1 mg/kg IV/IO (MAX 50 mg) OR Famotidine 0.25 mg/kg IV (MAX 20 mg) or Ranitidine 1 mg/kg IV
  • Consider echocardiography to rule out other causes
